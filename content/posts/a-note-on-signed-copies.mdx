The book lives in more than one place now. Kindle. Paperback. Shops like Centralbooks. That still startles me, in a quiet way: a story I once kept in a document, traveling without me.

But there is another way I like the book to travel — from my hands to yours.

If you would like a **signed copy from the author**, send a message on Instagram. If you are from the Philippines, please say so; I will do my best to make it simple. I cannot promise a grand production. I can promise that I will write in the front of the book the way I write everything else: a little imperfect, and sincere.

Thank you for reading. Thank you for finding the work at all. I used to dream of sharing this passion with as many people as I could. You, here, are already part of that dream — the one I did not lose.
